📘 How to Use This Tool

💰Don't know wholesale price? Toggle to "I know my retail price" — we'll estimate the wholesale price for you.
🔍Leave any field empty and check "Auto-fill" — we'll use historical data from your county.
📍Select your county — prices vary significantly between regions like Trans-Nzoia (surplus) and Nairobi (deficit).
📦Enter the supply volume available in your market. High supply typically means lower prices.
🌡Use the current temperature and rainfall from your local weather app or Kenya Meteorological Department.
📊The result shows a confidence range ±8% — plan buying/selling within this band.

🗓 Kenya Maize Price Calendar

🌱Mar–May (Long Rains): Planting season. Prices usually high due to low stocks from previous year.
🌾Jul–Aug (Harvest): Prices drop as new maize enters market — good time to buy.
🌧Oct–Dec (Short Rains): Second planting. Prices moderate and stabilising.
📈Jan–Feb (Lean Season): Stocks running low — prices tend to rise. Good time to sell stored maize.
